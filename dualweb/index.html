What is Box PvP?

Everyone spawns in the same box. Mine the blocks around you for resources, upgrade your armour and weapons, and take on anyone who gets in your way.

No long walks to find a fight. The action starts the second you join, and the better your gear gets, the bigger the target on your back.

How to join

  1. Open Minecraft and choose Multiplayer.
  2. Click Add Server and give it a name.
  3. Enter play.dualmines.net as the address.
  4. Press Done, then double-click Dualmines to join.
