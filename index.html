Box PvP

What is Dualmines Box PvP?

Game mode guide • 2 min read

Everyone spawns in the same box. Mine the blocks around you for resources, upgrade your armour and weapons, and fight anyone who gets in your way. No long walks to find action. The fight starts the second you join.

Getting started

How to join Dualmines

Quick start • 1 min read

  1. Open Minecraft and choose Multiplayer.
  2. Click Add Server and give it a name.
  3. Enter play.dualmines.net as the address.
  4. Press Done, then double-click Dualmines to join.

Important

Server Guidelines

To keep our community safe and your account in good standing, please follow our guidelines. Guidelines may be updated periodically, so check back regularly to stay current. Thank you for your cooperation!

Discord Guidelines

  • Respect Others: No harassment, bullying, or hate speech based on identity or personal attributes.
  • No Spamming: Avoid excessive pings, wall-of-text messages, or repetitive content.
  • Keep it Legal: Do not share pirated content, malware, or illegal material.
  • NSFW Content: Keep all discussions and media in designated channels; no adult content in general areas.
  • No Self-Promotion: Do not share invite links or ads without explicit permission from staff.
  • Follow Channel Topics: Use specific channels for their intended purpose (e.g., bot commands in #bots).
  • Profile Standards: Ensure nicknames and avatars are appropriate and non-offensive.
  • Staff Discretion: Follow instructions from moderators; their decisions regarding rule interpretation are final.

Server Rules

  • Clean Chat: No swearing, profanity, or bypassing chat filters with symbols or creative spelling.
  • Keep it Respectful: No harassment, insults, or toxic behaviour toward other players.
  • No Hate Speech: Zero tolerance for discrimination based on race, gender, or identity.
  • No Spamming: Avoid repeating messages, using all caps, or flooding the chat.
  • No Inappropriate Content: Do not share NSFW links, images, or adult-themed discussions.
  • English Only: Please use English in global chat so moderators can ensure everyone is following the rules.
  • No Hacking: Use of modified clients, X-ray packs, or third-party software for an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited.
  • No Exploits: Do not abuse game glitches, bugs, or “duping” methods to gain items or bypass mechanics.
  • No Combat Logging: Do not disconnect during a fight to avoid losing gear or being defeated.
  • No Scripting: Automated bots, macros, or auto-clickers are not allowed.
